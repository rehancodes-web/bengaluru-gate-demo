    Materials guide

    Bengaluru's lakes and drains often carry untreated sewage: low dissolved oxygen, hydrogen sulphide, grease and garbage. That decides most material choices below.

    Rules that apply to every gate

    • Don't mix metals in water without isolation. Stainless bolted directly to carbon steel or aluminium corrodes the less noble metal. Use insulating washers and sleeves, or keep wet parts all stainless.
    • Never run stainless against stainless. Screws, pins and nuts gall and seize. Pair a hard stainless pin or screw with a bronze or self-lubricating bush.
    • Pickle and passivate every stainless weld. Heat tint left on welds rusts in lake water.
    • Keep electrics dry. Motors, panels and power units go above the highest recorded flood level plus 0.5 m; anything that can flood is IP68.
    • Design for the crew. Grease points, seal clamps and bushes must be reachable from a platform, and the leaf must be lockable for maintenance.

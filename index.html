How the model works

Storage that widens as it fills. Each side has a stage-area curve, so the same inflow raises a wide lake less than a narrow one. Water is tracked as volume and converted to level, which keeps the water balance exact. Default curves: lake 380 m² at the sill rising to 560 m² at the 2.20 m crest; drain 300 to 440 m². Import surveyed curves below to replace them.

Gate discharge. The gate is 2.0 m wide and lifts up to 0.50 m. With the tailwater below the gate lip the flow is free: Q = Cd × b × a × √(2g(hup − a/2)). When the tailwater covers the lip it is submerged: Q = Cd × b × a × √(2g(hup − hdown)). The model blends the two smoothly and reverses the flow when the drain is higher. Cd defaults to 0.62; enter a calibrated value from field measurements.

Rule-based controller. Flood mode starts at 1.60 m and ends at 1.55 m. Demand = lake inflow + (stored volume above 1.55 m) / 300 s. Receiving allowance = drain outflow − local inflow + (spare drain volume below 1.80 m) / 120 s. Target opening = demand limited by allowance, as a share of full-opening flow. The gate closes when the drain reaches 1.80 m or forward head falls below 0.05 m.

PI controller (optional). Release = inflow + 2.0 × level error + 0.01 × accumulated error, with the same downstream limits, anti-windup and commands limited to 4% per second. Compare it with the rule-based controller on the same storm and export both runs.

Actuator. Travel is 2% per second, slowing to 1.2% per second as the head across the gate rises from 1.0 to 1.5 m. Above 1.5 m the drive trips on torque rather than force the gate open, and stays tripped until the case is reset.

Fault detection. The controller keeps its own water-balance estimate of the lake. If the lake transmitter drifts more than 8 cm from that estimate, movement is suspended. A partial jam is flagged when the gate moves less than half as far as commanded over 10 seconds.

Rainfall. Choose steady inflow, a design storm (3 m³/s peak at 10 minutes), a cloudburst burst (+5 m³/s for 5 minutes), or import a measured inflow series.

Worked example. Select "Lake rising" and press Reset. Upstream 1.85 m, downstream 1.10 m and 0.50 m³/s inflow give the initial target of about 42%. Click Advance to see actual travel start at 2%.

Drive a bench servo from the simulator with an Arduino UNO R4 running GateCommissioning.ino. Needs Chrome or Edge on a desktop (Web Serial). The sketch only moves after you send ARM, limits speed to 2% per second and holds position if the link goes quiet for 3 seconds.

Which flood mechanisms can the outlet influence?

MechanismGate response and complementary works
Lake filling with a clear receiving channelMeter release within receiving capacity. Verify downstream peak, timing and duration.
Receiving river or drain already highLimit release and detect reversal. Continued local runoff requires detention, a verified discharge route or engineered pumping.
Blocked culvert / upstream street pondingInvestigate differential levels and inspect the reach. Clearing a restriction or improving inlets can restore capacity independently of gate control.
Rainfall exceeds combined lake and channel capacityTrack spill and warn operators. Assess additional detention, overflow routing and downstream warning.
Disconnected street basin, sewer surcharge or groundwater floodingA lake outlet has no direct control without a hydraulic connection. Investigate surface drainage, sewer capacity or groundwater pathways separately.
